## Hello World

Welcome to my corner of the internet.

This is my first blog post, written entirely in **Markdown** and published using **Jekyll**. I've spent years building, automating, troubleshooting and occasionally breaking things in various technical roles, and I've accumulated a growing collection of notes, scripts, experiments, and lessons learned along the way. Rather than letting those ideas disappear into notebooks and private repositories, I decided it was time to start sharing some of them publicly.

The idea for this blog came after a conversation with a friend and former colleague who maintains [his own technical blog](https://blog.argakiig.xyz/). Seeing the value of documenting projects, discoveries and even mistakes convinced me that I should do the same. Writing things down forces a level of clarity that often reveals gaps in understanding and it creates a reference that can be useful months or years later.

Most of the content here will focus on technology and the things I find interesting enough to investigate in my spare time. Topics will likely include:

* Python scripting and automation
* PowerShell and Windows administration
* Bash and Linux tooling
* Operating systems and systems internals
* Networking concepts and troubleshooting
* Homelab projects and self-hosting
* Cloud platforms and infrastructure
* Random technical rabbit holes that seem worth documenting

Some posts will be tutorials, some will be project write-ups and others may simply be notes from research I've done while trying to solve a problem. The goal isn't to be authoritative on every topic, but to document what I've learned and hopefully provide something useful to others who encounter similar challenges.

If nothing else, this blog will serve as a searchable archive of things I've figured out, forgotten and then had to learn again.

Thanks for stopping by.
